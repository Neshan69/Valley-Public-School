Executive Principal's Message

Gopal Krishna Shrestha

“When we laid the foundation stone in 2052 B.S., our dream wasn’t just to erect another brick structure. We wanted to build a sanctuary where education feels like an extension of home — where kindness precedes competition, and moral courage is cherished above mere report cards.”

Nestled amid the terraced hills of Lamatar, Valley Public School has stood steadfast for nearly thirty years. Our children do not breathe city fumes or navigate noisy ring-roads; they wake up to crisp Himalayan air, birdsong, and teachers who know each child by name, temper, and hidden brilliance.

Frequently Asked Admissions Questions

What are the school visiting and office hours?

Our administrative and admissions help desk operates Sunday through Friday from 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM. Saturday remains closed except during scheduled open house admissions days.

Which documents are required for new enrollment?

Parents should bring two passport-sized photographs of the student, a photocopy of the official birth certificate, previous grade marksheet / character certificate (for Grade 1 and above), and the completed campus enrollment form.

Are day-boarder facilities and hygienic hot lunch provided?

Yes! We maintain an in-house canteen preparing fresh, balanced, vegetarian and non-vegetarian mid-day snacks and hot lunches supervised for nutritional hygiene. Supervised afternoon study hours are also available.
